## Phase 3 — Experiment C: Point Cloud Filtering, Normal Orientation & Smoothing Ablation

- **Script:** `python scripts/run_experiment_c.py`
- **Git Commit:** `a37b99e` (on branch `feature/hologen-build`)
- **Input Scene:** `sample_02_aloe_indoor.jpg` (Middlebury Aloe tabletop plant with multi-layer leaf occlusions, preprocessed to $512 \times 443 = 226,816$ pixels, `depth_anything_v2_small` + bilateral refinement)
- **Visual Outputs:** Orthographic Front ($XY$) and Side Depth Profile ($ZY$) views + `.ply` clouds saved in `outputs/experiment_c/`

**Key Engineering Finding:** Statistical Outlier Removal (`3_statistical_outlier_removal`) cuts nearest-neighbor spacing standard deviation by **52.2%** ($0.01682 \rightarrow 0.00804$) while removing only 2.48% of points. Adding the **Normal Grazing-Angle Filter** (`5_sor_plus_normal_grazing_filter`) specifically strips 24,847 "flying veil" points along leaf occlusion boundaries where the surface normal is $>80^\circ$ from the camera ray, running $3.4\times$ faster than Radius Outlier Removal (`272.71 ms` vs `932.62 ms`).
